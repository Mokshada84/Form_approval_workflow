# Acme Corporation — Employee Expense Policy (rev. 2026-04)

## §1.1 Who may claim
Any employee, contractor or intern engaged by Acme may submit an expense claim for costs incurred wholly and necessarily in the course of their work. Claims from former employees must be submitted within 30 days of their last working day and are handled by Finance directly.

## §1.2 Submission deadline
Expenses must be claimed within 60 days of the date the cost was incurred. Claims older than 60 days require a written explanation and are approved at the Senior Manager's discretion. Claims older than 180 days will not be reimbursed under any circumstances.

## §1.3 Receipts
An itemised receipt is required for every expense of $25 or more. A card statement is not an itemised receipt: it shows the total but not what was bought. Where a receipt has been lost, the claimant must attach a signed missing-receipt declaration; no more than three such declarations may be submitted by one person in any rolling twelve-month period.

## §1.4 Currency
Claims must be submitted in US dollars. Where a cost was incurred in another currency, convert using the rate on the date of the transaction and attach evidence of the rate used. Do not use the rate on the date of the claim.

## §1.5 Splitting claims
A single expense must not be divided across multiple claim forms in order to fall below an approval threshold. Deliberate splitting is treated as a disciplinary matter, not an administrative error.

## §1.6 Personal and business costs combined
Where a single receipt covers both business and personal spending, claim only the business portion and mark clearly on the claim which lines are excluded.

## §2.1 Approval thresholds
Claims up to and including $1,000 require approval from the claimant's Manager. Claims above $1,000 require approval from the Manager and then a Senior Manager. Claims above $10,000 additionally require written Finance Director sign-off before payment is released.

## §2.2 Self-approval
No employee may approve their own claim, nor a claim submitted by their direct line manager. Where the ordinary approver is the claimant, the claim passes to another holder of the same role.

## §2.3 Cross-charging to another department
An expense may be charged to a department other than the claimant's own where the work was performed for that department. The receiving department's Manager must be the approver. Cross-charged claims must state the reason in the claim name or description.

## §2.4 Pre-approval for large commitments
Any single commitment above $5,000 must be pre-approved in writing before the cost is incurred. A claim for an un-pre-approved commitment above this amount may be rejected regardless of merit.

## §3.1 Air travel
Economy class is the standard for all flights under six hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights of six hours or more. Business class requires Senior Manager pre-approval and is not permitted on domestic routes under any circumstances. First class is never reimbursed.

## §3.2 Accommodation
Hotel accommodation is reimbursed up to $250 per night in major metropolitan areas and $160 per night elsewhere. Rates above these limits require a written justification, such as a conference venue or a demonstrable lack of availability. Minibar, in-room entertainment and spa charges are not reimbursable.

## §3.3 Ground transport
Standard-class rail, buses, ride-hailing and taxis are reimbursable. Vehicle hire is reimbursed at the compact category unless three or more colleagues are travelling together. Fines, penalty charges and parking tickets are never reimbursable.

## §3.4 Mileage
Use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at $0.58 per mile. Mileage claims must state the start and end point of each journey. Commuting between home and a normal place of work is not reimbursable.

## §3.5 Per diem for meals while travelling
Where an employee is travelling overnight on business, meals are reimbursed either against receipts or at a per diem of $75 per full day, but not both. The per diem is reduced to $40 on days of partial travel.

## §3.6 Companion travel
Costs for a spouse, partner, family member or any other companion accompanying an employee on business travel are never reimbursable, including any incremental room rate.

## §4.1 Team meals
A meal for Acme employees only is reimbursable up to $60 per head including tax and service. The claim must name every attendee. Meals for teams of more than twelve people require Senior Manager approval in advance.

## §4.2 Client entertainment
Entertainment of clients, prospective clients and business partners is reimbursable up to $150 per head including tax and service. The claim must name every attendee AND state the organisation each external attendee represents. A claim that names an external guest without identifying their organisation cannot be verified and will be returned.

## §4.3 Alcohol
Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a meal and only where clients are present. Alcohol at an employees-only event is not reimbursable. Spirits are not reimbursable in any circumstance.

## §4.4 Entertainment venues
Tickets to sporting events, concerts and theatre are reimbursable only where a client attends and only with Senior Manager pre-approval. Golf club membership, casino spending and adult entertainment are never reimbursable.

## §4.5 Gifts to clients
Gifts to clients or prospective clients are reimbursable up to $100 per recipient per year. Cash and cash equivalents, including gift cards and vouchers, are never reimbursable as gifts.

## §5.1 Laptops and primary devices
Acme issues one laptop per employee on a four-year replacement cycle. Early replacement requires evidence of failure or a documented change in role requirements. Employees may not purchase a laptop personally and claim it back without prior IT approval; devices bought this way cannot be enrolled in device management.

## §5.2 Peripherals and accessories
Monitors, keyboards, mice, docking stations, headsets and cables are reimbursable up to $600 per employee per rolling twelve months without further justification. Above that limit, IT Manager approval is required.

## §5.3 Mobile devices and connectivity
A mobile handset is reimbursed up to $900 on a three-year cycle. Monthly mobile plans are reimbursed up to $60 per month. Home broadband is reimbursed at 50% of the monthly cost for employees contractually designated as remote.

## §5.4 Software licences
Software must be procured through IT. Individually purchased subscriptions are reimbursable only where IT has confirmed in writing that no existing corporate licence covers the need. Any tool that will process client data requires a security review before purchase, regardless of cost.

## §5.5 Home office furniture
Desks, chairs and lighting are reimbursable up to $800 once every five years for employees contractually designated as remote. Items remain the property of Acme and must be returned or bought out at depreciated value on leaving.

## §6.1 External counsel
Engagement of external legal counsel requires prior approval from the Legal department regardless of amount. Invoices must be itemised by matter and by hourly rate. Block-billed invoices with no breakdown will be returned.

## §6.2 Filing and registry fees
Statutory filing fees, registry fees and court fees are reimbursable in full against official receipts. These are exempt from the $5,000 pre-approval requirement in §2.4 because the amounts are set externally and are not negotiable.

## §6.3 Compliance and regulatory costs
Costs of mandatory regulatory training, certification and licensing are reimbursable in full. Voluntary professional memberships are reimbursable up to $500 per year with Manager approval.

## §6.4 Contract review by third parties
Third-party contract review is reimbursable only where the Legal department has confirmed it lacks capacity. The confirmation must be referenced in the claim.

## §7.1 Training and conferences
Course fees, conference tickets and examination fees are reimbursable with Manager approval. Where the total cost including travel exceeds $3,000, Senior Manager approval is required. An employee leaving within twelve months of a course costing over $2,000 may be asked to repay a pro-rated share.

## §7.2 Office supplies
Routine office supplies are reimbursable without itemisation up to $75 per claim. Above that, an itemised receipt is required under §1.3.

## §7.3 Charitable and political contributions
Charitable donations made on Acme's behalf require Finance Director approval. Political contributions of any kind are never reimbursable and must never be claimed.

## §7.4 Expenses that are never reimbursable
The following are never reimbursable under any circumstance: traffic and parking fines; personal grooming; personal travel insurance; loyalty programme fees; childcare; pet care; personal subscriptions and streaming services; and any cost for which the employee has already been reimbursed by a third party.
